Delete Your Muzzomo Account

Account and Data Request

Muzzomo is a home service platform connecting users with trusted professionals. If you wish to delete your account and associated data, please follow the steps below.

Important: Account deletion must be completed inside the Muzzomo mobile app, as it requires you to be signed in securely to verify your identity and protect your account.

How to Delete Your Account

To delete your Muzzomo account and associated data, please follow these steps:

1

Open the Muzzomo mobile app.

2

Sign in to your account.

3

On the Home screen, tap your profile avatar in the app bar.

4

In the drawer menu, tap your profile avatar.

5

Your Profile page will open.

6

Tap the Edit Profile button.

7

Scroll to the bottom of the Edit Profile page.

8

Tap Delete Account.

9

The Delete Account page will open.

10

Follow the instructions on that page to permanently delete your account and associated data.

What Data Is Deleted

✓ Data Deleted

When you delete your account, Muzzomo will delete or deactivate your account profile and associated personal data, including profile information, contact information, saved addresses, service requests, bookings, and related account data, subject to any legal, security, payment, fraud-prevention, or compliance retention requirements.

⚠ Data That May Be Retained

Some records may be retained for a limited period where required for legal, accounting, payment, security, dispute resolution, or compliance purposes.
